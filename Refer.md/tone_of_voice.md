# Lokavia International — Tone of Voice Guide

**Naming note:** "Lokavia International" is the legal entity name. Website copy, general "we" voice, and brand mentions should use **"Lokavia"** only — reserve the full legal name for footer copyright, legal pages, and formal export documents where legal precision is required.

## Core Personality

**Trustworthy & reliable** + **Ambitious & globally scaled** — every piece of copy should make a buyer feel two things simultaneously: *this company will deliver exactly what it commits to*, and *this company thinks and operates at an international scale, not a local one.*

**Voice/perspective:** "We" — Lokavia International speaks as a collective institutional voice, not as an individual founder. (Exception: personal LinkedIn profile, which is deliberately founder-led — handled separately from site copy.)

**Formality:** Professional but approachable — confident, plain-spoken, never stiff corporate-speak, never casual/slangy. Restrained wit is welcome in the right channel (see below) — never in documents.

---

## Channel Calibration

| Channel | Tone | Humor allowed? |
|---|---|---|
| Export documents (PI, COA, packing list, spec sheets, contracts) | Strictly formal, precise, zero personality | No |
| Buyer emails / outreach | Professional-approachable, warm, direct | No |
| Company Profile / Catalogue | Formal-leaning, professional-but-approachable | Very light, only if it reads as confident restraint, not a joke |
| **Website copy** | **Confident, plain-spoken** | **Subtle wit in taglines/microcopy only** |
| LinkedIn company posts | Most personality of any channel | Yes — restrained, industry-aware wit |

**Rule of thumb:** the closer the content is to a legal/compliance artifact, the less personality it carries. The closer it is to a public-facing relationship-building channel, the more room there is.

**For website copy specifically:** confident and plain-spoken is the baseline across every page. Subtle wit is permitted only in taglines and microcopy (e.g., button hover text, empty-state messages) — never in spec tables, forms, legal pages, or anywhere resembling a compliance artifact.

---

## Do

- Make claims specific and verifiable ("60-day delivery lead time," not "fast and reliable")
- Write short, direct sentences — clarity signals confidence
- Use trade terminology correctly and without over-explaining it (buyers know what FOB, MOQ, and Incoterms mean)
- Let ambition show through scale of thinking (global reach, category expansion) rather than hype adjectives
- Use dry, understated wit sparingly in LinkedIn/website copy — a knowing aside, not a punchline

## Don't

- Never use humor, wordplay, or casual asides in any document a buyer signs, files, or submits to customs
- Never oversell with superlatives ("world's best," "unmatched quality," "revolutionary") — trust brands earn claims through specificity, not adjectives
- Never use slang, emojis, or exclamation marks in formal copy
- Never apologize for being a new/young company — state facts plainly and let the work speak
- Never let "approachable" drift into "casual" — there's a difference between warm and unserious

## Example: Same Message, Calibrated by Channel

**Export document (COA cover):**
Lokavia International certifies that the goods described herein meet the specifications stated in the attached Certificate of Analysis.

**Buyer email:**
Attached is the COA for your review. Let us know if you'd like any additional testing documentation before we proceed.

**Company Profile:**
Lokavia International exists to make cross-border sourcing simpler for the buyers who depend on it — starting with agri-commodities, built to scale beyond them.

**LinkedIn post:**
Export paperwork has a reputation for being painful. We happen to enjoy it — mostly because getting it right the first time is how shipments actually move.

Same brand, same "we," same underlying confidence — the wit only shows up in the last one.

---

## Application to Website Content

- **Homepage, Products, Quality & Sourcing:** confident, plain-spoken, factual — no adjectives-only claims, specificity over emotion
- **About/Vision section:** restrained ambition — state trajectory as fact ("expanding into additional agri-commodity categories"), not as a declared claim to greatness. Never apologize for being newly registered; state facts plainly.
- **RFQ/Contact page:** closer to the "buyer email" register — professional-approachable, direct, zero wit
- **Taglines/microcopy only** (hero headline, button labels, empty states): subtle, restrained wit is acceptable if it reads as confident restraint, not a joke
