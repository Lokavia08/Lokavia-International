## What Key Specifications Determine Garlic Powder Quality?

When sourcing bulk [Dehydrated Garlic Powder](/products/garlic-powder) from India for food manufacturing, seasoning blends, or ready-to-eat meals, specify these technical parameters:

| Parameter | Standard Spec | Premium / SO₂-Free Spec |
|---|---|---|
| Moisture Content | ≤6.0% | ≤5.0% |
| Particle Size (Mesh) | 80 mesh (fine) | 100–120 mesh (ultra-fine) |
| Total Ash | ≤5.0% | ≤4.5% |
| Acid Insoluble Ash | ≤0.5% | ≤0.3% |
| SO₂ (Sulphur Dioxide) | ≤2,000 ppm | SO₂-Free (<10 ppm) |
| Allicin Content | ≥0.3% | ≥0.5% |
| Shelf Life | 24 months | 18–24 months |

## Mesh Sizes Explained: Which Grade Suits Your Application?

![Dehydrated Garlic Powder 80 Mesh vs 100 Mesh Quality Inspection](/blog/blog-garlic-mesh.jpg)

- **60 Mesh (Coarse):** Used in dry rub seasonings, meat processing, and industrial spice blends where visible garlic speckling is preferred.
- **80 Mesh (Standard Fine):** The global standard for food manufacturing, instant soups, gravies, and snack coatings.
- **100–120 Mesh (Ultra-Fine):** Dissolves rapidly in liquid formulations, sauces, salad dressings, and beverage blends.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is the minimum allicin level required for high-potency garlic powder?**
High-potency garlic powder should contain a minimum of 0.3% to 0.5% allicin by weight. Allicin provides the distinctive pungent aroma and active bioactive benefits.

**Q: Why is moisture control critical during bulk container transport?**
Garlic powder is highly hygroscopic. If moisture exceeds 6%, the powder can form hard clumps during ocean transit. Lokavia packages garlic powder in heavy-gauge moisture-barrier HDPE liners inside corrugated master cartons.

**Q: How does Indian garlic powder compare to Chinese garlic powder in pungency?**
Indian garlic varieties naturally possess higher volatile oil and allicin content due to climate conditions in Madhya Pradesh, providing stronger flavor intensity per kilogram. See our [direct origin sourcing hubs](/quality-sourcing).

**Q: Can Lokavia supply customized mesh sizes for specialized food processors?**
Yes. In addition to standard 80 mesh, custom milling to 60 mesh, 100 mesh, or 120 mesh is available for volume procurement orders. Read more in our [frequently asked questions section](/faq).
