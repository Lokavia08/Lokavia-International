## Technical Specifications for Bulk Garlic Powder

When sourcing bulk [Dehydrated Garlic Powder](/products/garlic-powder) from India for food manufacturing, seasoning blends, or ready meals, specify these technical parameters:

| Parameter | Standard Spec | Premium / SO₂-Free Spec |
|---|---|---|
| **Moisture Content** | ≤ 6.0% | ≤ 5.0% |
| **Particle Size (Mesh)** | 80 mesh (Standard fine) | 100–120 mesh (Ultra-fine) |
| **Total Ash** | ≤ 5.0% | ≤ 4.5% |
| **Acid Insoluble Ash** | ≤ 0.5% | ≤ 0.3% |
| **SO₂ (Sulphur Dioxide)** | ≤ 2,000 ppm | SO₂-Free (< 10 ppm) |
| **Allicin Content** | ≥ 0.3% | ≥ 0.5% (High potency) |
| **Shelf Life** | 18–24 months | 24 months |

## Mesh Sizes Explained

![Dehydrated Garlic Powder 80 Mesh vs 100 Mesh Quality Inspection](/blog/blog-garlic-mesh.jpg)

- **60 Mesh (Coarse):** Used in dry rub seasonings, meat processing, and industrial spice blends where visible garlic speckling is preferred.
- **80 Mesh (Standard Fine):** The global standard for food manufacturing, instant soups, gravies, and snack coatings.
- **100–120 Mesh (Ultra-Fine):** Dissolves rapidly in liquid formulations, sauces, salad dressings, and beverage blends.

## SO₂-Free vs. Standard Dehydrated Garlic Powder

European Union buyers increasingly require **SO₂-free garlic powder**. Standard dehydration processes use sulfur dioxide treatment to preserve natural cream-white color and extend storage life.

SO₂-free processing relies strictly on controlled temperature air-drying and hygienic handling without additive preservatives.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the minimum allicin level required for high-potency garlic powder?
High-potency garlic powder should contain a minimum of 0.3% to 0.5% allicin potential by weight. Allicin provides the distinctive pungent aroma and active bioactive benefits.

### Why is moisture control critical during bulk container transport?
Garlic powder is highly hygroscopic. If moisture exceeds 6.0%, the powder can form hard clumps during ocean transit. Lokavia packages garlic powder in heavy-gauge moisture-barrier HDPE liners inside corrugated master cartons.

### How does Indian garlic powder compare to other origins in pungency?
Indian garlic varieties (cultivated primarily in Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat) naturally possess higher volatile oil and allicin content due to semi-arid agro-climatic conditions, providing stronger flavor intensity per kilogram.

### Can Lokavia supply customized mesh sizes for specialized food processors?
Yes. In addition to standard 80 mesh, custom milling to 60 mesh, 100 mesh, or 120 mesh is available for volume procurement orders.
