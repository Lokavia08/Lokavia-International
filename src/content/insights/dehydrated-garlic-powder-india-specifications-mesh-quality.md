**TL;DR:** Food-grade Indian garlic powder must maintain moisture <6%, ash content <5%, and pass heavy metal and pesticide residue (MRL) testing. Premium grades specify 80–100 mesh particle size and high allicin content for flavor potency. Always request a batch-specific COA and verify SO₂-free status for EU shipments.

---

## What Key Specifications Determine Garlic Powder Quality?

When sourcing bulk garlic powder from India for food manufacturing, seasoning blends, or ready-to-eat meals, specify these technical parameters:

| Parameter | Standard Spec | Premium / SO₂-Free Spec |
|---|---|---|
| Moisture Content | ≤6.0% | ≤5.0% |
| Particle Size (Mesh) | 80 mesh (fine) | 100–120 mesh (ultra-fine) |
| Total Ash | ≤5.0% | ≤4.5% |
| Acid Insoluble Ash | ≤0.5% | ≤0.3% |
| SO₂ (Sulphur Dioxide) | ≤2,000 ppm | SO₂-Free (<10 ppm) |
| Allicin Content | ≥0.3% | ≥0.5% |
| Shelf Life | 24 months | 18–24 months |

---

## Mesh Sizes Explained: Which Grade Suits Your Application?

- **60 Mesh (Coarse):** Used in dry rub seasonings, meat processing, and industrial spice blends where visible garlic speckling is preferred.
- **80 Mesh (Standard Fine):** The global standard for food manufacturing, instant soups, gravies, and snack coatings.
- **100–120 Mesh (Ultra-Fine):** Dissolves rapidly in liquid formulations, sauces, salad dressings, and beverage blends.

---

## SO₂-Free vs. Standard Dehydrated Garlic Powder

European Union buyers increasingly require **SO₂-free garlic powder**. Standard dehydration processes use sulphur dioxide treatment to preserve natural cream-white color and extend storage life.

SO₂-free processing relies strictly on controlled temperature air-drying and hygienic handling without additive preservatives. While slightly more costly, it complies fully with clean-label and EU organic standards.
