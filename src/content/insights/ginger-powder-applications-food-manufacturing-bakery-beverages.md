## 1. Bakery Applications: Specifications and Dosing

Bakery represents the largest volume application for dehydrated ginger powder. Ginger provides characteristic warmth, pungent aroma, and natural preservative qualities to baked goods.

### Formulation Guidelines for Baked Goods

| Product Type | Recommended Mesh | Gingerol Content | Typical Dosage | Processing Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ginger Snaps & Cookies** | 60–80 mesh | 0.8% – 1.1% | 8–12 g / kg flour | Coarser mesh provides visible speck appeal |
| **Ginger Cakes & Muffins** | 80–100 mesh | 1.0% – 1.2% | 5–10 g / kg flour | Blend with dry flour mix for uniform dispersion |
| **Gingerbread Loaves** | 80 mesh | 1.1% – 1.4% | 12–18 g / kg flour | Higher dosage offsets fermentation aroma loss |
| **Artisan Yeast Breads** | 60–80 mesh | 1.2% – 1.5% | 6–10 g / kg flour | Two-stage addition: 70% in dough, 30% surface dust |

### Bakery Processing Steps:
1. **Dry Flour Blending:** Premix ginger powder with dry flour, leavening agents, and spices (cinnamon, nutmeg, clove) to prevent lump formation.
2. **Creaming Phase:** For maximum aroma impact, fold 20–30% of the ginger dose into the fat/sugar cream.
3. **Baking Stability:** Gingerol compounds remain stable at standard 175°C (350°F) baking temperatures, with minor conversion into aromatic shogaols.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Why does ginger powder sometimes taste bitter or harsh?
Harshness occurs if raw ginger rhizomes were dried at excessively high temperatures or aged beyond two years. Lokavia uses controlled low-temperature belt dehydration (50°C–55°C) to preserve delicate volatile oils and eliminate burnt notes.

### Can the same ginger powder grade be used for bakery and beverages?
Particle size differs: bakery formulations favor 60–80 mesh for visible speck appeal and dough texture, while beverages require 100–120 micro-fine mesh to prevent rapid bottle sedimentation.

### How do beverage manufacturers prevent ginger sediment in bottles?
In liquid beverages, pre-blending fine ginger powder with a small fraction (0.5%–1.0%) of food-grade hydrocolloids like gum arabic or xanthan gum keeps particles uniformly suspended.

### What is the shelf life of bulk ginger powder?
Stored in cool, dry conditions below 22°C and ≤55% relative humidity in sealed poly-lined kraft bags, dehydrated ginger powder maintains full potency for 18 to 24 months.

### Can Lokavia provide steam-sterilized ginger powder for sensitive formulations?
Yes. We supply steam-sterilized ginger powder with Total Plate Counts (TPC) below 5,000 CFU/g, ideal for clean-label functional foods, infant nutrition, and nutraceutical products.
