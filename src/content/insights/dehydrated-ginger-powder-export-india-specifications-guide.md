## How Does Origin Affect Gingerol Content and Essential Oil Levels?

![Dehydrated Ginger Powder Sourcing and Processing](/blog/blog-ginger-cover.jpg)

India's primary ginger growing belts produce distinctly different flavor profiles suited to specialized industrial applications:

- **Kerala (Malabar Ginger):** Renowned globally for high volatile oil levels (1.5% to 2.2%) and deep gingerol pungency. Highly preferred by beverage bottlers, chai spice compounders, and pharmaceutical extractors.
- **Assam & North East Belt:** Characterized by low fiber content, intense heat, and high yield. Ideal for industrial bakery mixes, curry powder blending, and commercial sauce manufacturing.

## Which Mesh Size Is Best for Your Manufacturing Application?

Selecting the correct particle size ensures uniform dissolution, optimal extraction, and consistent mouthfeel across your production lines:

- **40–60 Mesh (Coarse Milled):** Used in herbal tea bags, infusion blends, and botanical extraction processes where rapid liquid filtration is required without dust carryover.
- **80 Mesh (Standard Fine):** The global benchmark for commercial bakery products (ginger snaps, biscuits), dry spice rubs, instant soup mixes, and snack seasonings.
- **100 Mesh (Ultra-Fine):** Designed for rapid cold-water dissolution in ready-to-drink beverages, sauces, marinades, and nutraceutical dietary capsules.
