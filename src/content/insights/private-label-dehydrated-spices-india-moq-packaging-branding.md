## Packaging Engineering & Barrier Technologies

Dehydrated alliums and powdered spices are highly hygroscopic. Selecting appropriate barrier laminates guarantees an **18 to 24-month retail shelf life**:

* **Multi-Layer Doypacks:** Standard structures use `PET (12µ) / Met-PET (12µ) / Polyethylene (60–80µ)` or `Kraft Paper (50g) / ALU Foil (7µ) / PE (60µ)`. These achieve water vapor transmission rates under $0.5\text{ g/m}^2/\text{day}$ and oxygen transmission rates under $0.5\text{ cm}^3/\text{m}^2/\text{day}$.
* **Recyclable Mono-Materials:** MDO-PE / PE-EVOH combinations designed for Code 4 LDPE recycling streams.
* **Rigid Glass & PET Shakers:** Dual-flap shaker caps with induction heat-sealed (IHS) foil liners ensure hermetic tamper evidence.
* **Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP):** Automated nitrogen gas flushing reduces residual headspace oxygen below 1.0%, preventing oxidative discoloration and flavor loss during ocean transit.

## Regulatory & Labeling Compliance

Selling branded food products internationally requires strict adherence to destination label standards:

| Regulation Standard | Target Region | Critical Packaging Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **US FDA 21 CFR Part 101** | United States | Dual-unit net quantity (g/oz), Nutrition Facts Panel, FALCPA/FASTER allergen declarations, US importer of record. |
| **EU Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011** | European Union & UK | Metric net weight with $e$-mark, minimum font x-height ($1.2\text{ mm}$), multilingual translations if distributed across member states. |
| **GSO Standard 9/2013 & 150/2013** | GCC (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman) | Arabic & English bilingual labeling, production/expiry dates (MM/YYYY), Halal certification registry details. |
| **FSANZ Standard** | Australia & New Zealand | Country of Origin Labelling (CoOL) bar chart / kangaroo logo standard, Plain English Allergen Labelling (PEAL). |

### Key Label Components Required on Retail Packs:
- **Principal Display Panel (Front):** Brand logo, product name, net weight (metric & imperial), organic/halal badges, country of origin ("India").
- **Information Panel (Back/Side):** Single or blended ingredient statement, standardized nutrition table, allergen advisory, lot/batch number, expiry date, UPC/EAN barcode, and importer/distributor contact.

## End-to-End Private Label Workflow

1. **Formulation & Mesh Selection:** Define particle size (Flakes, Minced, Granules, 80–100 Mesh Powder) and chemical parameters (Moisture ≤ 5.0%, Volatile Oil, Allicin).
2. **Packaging Dieline & Artwork:** Buyer supplies vector artwork on dieline templates for regulatory review and prepress proofing.
3. **Pre-Production Sampling & COA:** Courier-dispatched physical lab samples and ISO 17025 accredited laboratory analysis for organoleptic sign-off.
4. **Cylinder Engraving & Automated Packing:** Gravure printing, film laminating, automated filling, nitrogen flushing, and induction seal verification.
5. **Quality Control & Master Cartoning:** In-line check-weighing, metal detection (Fe 1.0mm, Non-Fe 1.2mm, SS 1.5mm), export master cartons, and pallet stretch-wrapping.
6. **Export Clearance & Ocean Dispatch:** Phytosanitary inspection, Certificate of Origin, and ocean container shipment under FOB, CIF, or CFR terms.

Yes. In addition to pure single-ingredient dehydrated alliums and spices, we formulate custom dry rubs, seasoned salts, BBQ blends, and functional herbal powders according to your proprietary specifications under strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA).
