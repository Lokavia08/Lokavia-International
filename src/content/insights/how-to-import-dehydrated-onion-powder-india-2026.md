## Core Specifications for Your Purchase Order

| Parameter | Standard Export Range | Commercial Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Moisture** | ≤ 6.0% | Essential for shelf life and caking prevention |
| **Mesh Size** | 60–120 mesh (80 mesh standard) | Dictates solubility and application texture |
| **Pungency** | ≥ 30 μmol/g (pyruvate equivalent) | Measures authentic savory allium flavor strength |
| **Ash Content** | ≤ 5.0% | Indicates raw washing and processing cleanliness |
| **Microbial Limits** | TPC < 10,000 CFU/g | Meets US and EU food safety standards |
| **Sulfite (SO₂)** | ≤ 2,000 ppm or SO₂-Free | European buyers often specify non-sulfited clean label |

## Mandatory Export Documents

1. **Commercial Invoice & Packing List:** Exact carton counts, weights, batch codes, and HS codes (071220).
2. **Certificate of Origin (CoO):** Issued by Chamber of Commerce / APEDA.
3. **Phytosanitary Certificate:** Issued by India's Plant Quarantine Organization.
4. **Certificate of Analysis (COA):** NABL-accredited independent lab test report per batch.
5. **Bill of Lading (B/L):** Official title document from the shipping carrier.

## Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ) & Incoterms

- **Standard MOQ:** 2,000 kg (1 pallet equivalent).
- **Full Container Load (FCL):** 14,000–18,000 kg (20ft container) or up to 25,000 kg (40ft container).
- **Recommended Incoterms:** **FOB Mundra/Nhava Sheva** for buyers with corporate freight forwarders, or **CIF named port** for all-inclusive delivery.
