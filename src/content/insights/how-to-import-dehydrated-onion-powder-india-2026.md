## Why Do Buyers Source Dehydrated Onion Powder from India?

India produces over 1.5 million metric tonnes of onions annually. Gujarat — specifically the Mahuva and Bhavnagar districts — processes the majority of export-grade dehydrated onion. Key reasons buyers prefer India:

- **Year-round supply** due to two harvest cycles (kharif and rabi)
- **Cost advantage**: 30–40% lower FOB pricing than China or Egypt
- **Processing quality**: hot-air dehydration plants producing consistent mesh sizes
- **Regulatory readiness**: FSSAI, APEDA, and third-party lab-tested consignments

## What Specifications Should You Specify in Your Purchase Order?

Before requesting a quote, confirm these parameters with your supplier:

| Parameter | Standard Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Moisture | ≤6% | Critical for shelf life |
| Mesh size | 60–120 mesh | 80 mesh = most common for powder |
| Pungency | ≥30 μmol/g (fresh weight equivalent) | Relevant for flavor-critical applications |
| Ash content | ≤5% | Indicates processing cleanliness |
| Microbial limits | TPC <10,000 CFU/g | Per EU/US import standards |
| Sulphur dioxide (SO₂) | ≤2,000 ppm or SO₂-free | EU buyers often require SO₂-free |

Always request a **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** matching these parameters before confirming an order.

## Which Certifications Should Your Supplier Have?

| Certification | Required For |
|---|---|
| FSSAI | All Indian food exports (mandatory) |
| APEDA registration | All agri-commodity exports from India |
| Halal | UAE, Saudi Arabia, Malaysia, Indonesia |
| Kosher | USA, Israel, select EU markets |
| ISO 22000 / HACCP | EU, USA — food safety management systems |
| USFDA registration | Mandatory for all US-bound food shipments |

Ask for certificate numbers and verify them directly on the issuing body's website.

## What Export Documents Will You Receive?

A standard export shipment from India includes:

1. **Commercial Invoice** — price, quantity, buyer/seller details
2. **Packing List** — carton count, net/gross weight, dimensions
3. **Certificate of Origin** — issued by APEDA or Chamber of Commerce
4. **Phytosanitary Certificate** — issued by Plant Quarantine Authority of India
5. **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** — third-party lab report per batch
6. **Bill of Lading / Airway Bill** — shipping document from carrier
7. **Health Certificate** — for EU and select markets

For the US: add **FCE/SID registration** if the product is an acidified food. For the EU: add an **Entry Document** at the port of entry.

## What Is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

- **Standard MOQ:** 2,000 kg (one pallet equivalent)
- **Full Container Load (FCL):** 18,000–20,000 kg (20-ft container) or 25,000 kg (40-ft container)
- **Trial orders:** Some exporters accept 500 kg–1,000 kg for initial quality verification

## What Incoterm Should You Use?

| Incoterm | Who handles freight & insurance | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **FOB** (Free On Board) | Buyer from port of origin | Buyers with established freight forwarders |
| **CIF** (Cost, Insurance, Freight) | Seller to destination port | First-time importers who want simplicity |
| **DDP** (Delivered Duty Paid) | Seller handles everything | Buyers who want zero logistics management |

FOB is the most common for bulk ingredient imports from India. It gives the buyer full control over freight costs and insurance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I request a custom mesh size?**
Yes. Most Indian processors offer 40, 60, 80, 100, and 120 mesh. Minimum batch sizes for custom mesh are typically 1,000 kg.

**Q: Is SO₂-free onion powder available from India?**
Yes. SO₂-free product is processed without sulphur dioxide treatment. It has a shorter shelf life (12–18 months vs. 24 months) and slightly higher price. Specify at the time of enquiry.

**Q: What packaging is standard for bulk export?**
25 kg HDPE bags (multi-layer, moisture-proof) inside fibreboard cartons. Vacuum-sealed options available on request for premium grades.

**Q: Is dehydrated onion powder the same as onion powder sold in retail?**
No. Bulk export-grade onion powder is an industrial ingredient. Retail packets use the same raw material but are re-processed and packaged for consumers.
