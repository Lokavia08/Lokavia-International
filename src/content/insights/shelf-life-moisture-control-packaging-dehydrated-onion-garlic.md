## Why Moisture Control Is Critical for Dehydrated Alliums

Dehydrated onion and garlic powders are highly hygroscopic due to natural water-soluble fructan sugars. Exposure to relative humidity (RH) above 60% causes rapid moisture absorption, triggering several quality issues:

1. **Caking & Clumping:** Moisture plasticizes powder particle surfaces, lowering glass transition temperature ($T_g$) and forming hard lumps that obstruct automated factory dosers.
2. **Maillard Browning:** Free sugars react in the presence of excess moisture (>7.0%), discoloring pure white onion powder into an unappealing yellow or dark brown hue.
3. **Allicin & Volatile Degradation:** Unwanted moisture triggers premature alliinase enzymatic breakdown, reducing aromatic pungency prior to final consumption.
4. **Microbial Proliferation:** Maintaining moisture ≤6.0% keeps water activity ($a_w < 0.50$), suppressing bacterial, mold, and yeast reproduction.

## Global Export Packaging Standards

1. **25 kg Multi-Wall Kraft Bags with Double Poly Liners:**
   - 3-ply heavy-duty natural brown Kraft paper bag.
   - Double virgin food-grade LDPE liners (80–100 microns each), individually twisted, folded, and heat-sealed or cable-tied.
   - Ideal for food processing plants and commercial spice blending.

2. **20 kg / 25 kg Corrugated Master Cartons with Aluminum Foil Bags:**
   - Heavy-duty 5-ply double-wall corrugated cardboard box (140–180 gsm kraft linerboard).
   - High-barrier laminated aluminum foil inner liner (PET/AL/PE), providing near-zero moisture transmission ($\text{MVTR} < 0.05\text{ g/m}^2/\text{day}$).
   - Ideal for humid tropical destinations and extended storage up to 24–36 months.

3. **500 kg – 1000 kg Bulk FIBC Big Bags:**
   - Woven polypropylene (PP) bulk bags with corner lifting loops and form-fitted 100-micron gusseted moisture liners.
   - Designed for automated industrial repacking lines and commercial sauce facilities.

## Warehouse Storage & Ocean Shipping Protection

* **Storage Conditions:** Store between 15°C and 22°C (59°F–72°F) at relative humidity below 60% RH. Keep away from direct sunlight.
* **Pallet Spacing:** Store on ISPM-15 certified heat-treated pallets elevated 15 cm off floors and 50 cm away from masonry walls.
* **Ocean Transit Protection:** Lokavia installs 2.0 kg calcium chloride ($\text{CaCl}_2$) container desiccant poles per 20ft container to prevent condensation ("container rain") during ocean freight.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What happens if dehydrated onion powder moisture exceeds 7.0% during storage?
When moisture exceeds 7.0%, the powder reaches its critical water activity threshold ($a_w > 0.50$). This triggers particle caking, rapid Maillard browning (darkening), and loss of volatile pungency. Symmetrical moisture-barrier poly liners prevent ambient moisture absorption.

### How long does bulk dehydrated garlic powder stay fresh after opening?
Once outer export packaging is opened, garlic powder should be re-sealed tightly in its inner poly liner and consumed within 30 to 60 days under humidity-controlled (<60% RH) warehouse conditions to prevent clumping.

### What is the main difference between standard double poly liners and aluminum foil barrier bags?
Standard double poly liners (80–100 μm LDPE) provide excellent water vapor protection for 18–24 months under standard ambient transport. Aluminum foil laminated bags provide near-impermeable zero-MVTR barrier protection, ideal for high-humidity sea routes or SO₂-free clean-label products requiring a 24–36 month shelf life.

### How do container desiccants protect dehydrated Alliums during ocean freight?
Calcium chloride container desiccants absorb airborne moisture inside sealed sea containers during temperature swings, preventing condensation ("container rain") from dripping onto master corrugated cartons.
