## Why Export Documentation Compliance Matters in Agri-Trade

Agricultural trade is subject to strict sanitary and phytosanitary (SPS) regulations enforced by importing authorities such as the US FDA, EFSA, and GCC customs authorities. Every commercial shipment must be accompanied by verified, traceable export documentation issued by accredited Indian bodies and certified laboratories.

## Mandatory Export Documentation Checklist

| Document Name | Issuing / Managing Authority | Purpose & Importer Audit Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** | NABL Accredited Lab (Eurofins, SGS) | Verifies physical mesh size, moisture content, ash, allicin/volatile oils, heavy metals, and pesticide MRL clearance. |
| **Phytosanitary Certificate** | Plant Quarantine Organization of India | Certifies the shipment is free from quarantine pests, soil contaminants, and plant diseases. |
| **Certificate of Origin (CoO)** | Chamber of Commerce / Export Inspection Council | Verifies Indian origin for preferential tariff treatment under FTAs and customs valuation. |
| **FSSAI Health Certificate** | Food Safety and Standards Authority of India | Confirms the manufacturing facility complies with Indian food safety standards and HACCP guidelines. |

## 1. Auditing the Certificate of Analysis (COA)

* **Physical & Chemical Parameters:** Moisture max 6.0% for [Onion Powder](/products/onion-powder) and [Garlic Powder](/products/garlic-powder); max 9.0% for [Ginger Powder](/products/ginger-powder). Total ash ≤5.0%, Acid Insoluble Ash ≤0.5%.
* **Pesticide MRLs & Heavy Metals:** Screened via GC-MS/MS and LC-MS/MS against EU Regulation (EC) No 396/2005 and US EPA tolerances. Lead (<0.5 ppm), Cadmium (<0.2 ppm), Arsenic (<0.5 ppm), Mercury (<0.05 ppm).
* **Microbiological Safety:** Salmonella (Absent in 25g/375g), E. coli (Absent in 10g), Total Plate Count (<100,000 CFU/g).

---

## 2. Phytosanitary Certificate & Fumigation Protocols

* **Container Fumigation:** Shipments undergo in-container fumigation using **Aluminum Phosphide (ALP)** or **Methyl Bromide (MB)** as mandated by destination quarantine rules.
* **Fumigation Certificate:** Issued by accredited pest control operators detailing dosage, exposure period, and degassing verification.
* **Endorsement:** The Phytosanitary Certificate officially records the treatment details.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### When are pre-shipment documents shared with the buyer?
Lokavia provides digital pre-shipment document sets (draft B/L, batch COA, commercial invoice, packing list) 5 to 7 days prior to vessel arrival, allowing your customs broker to file entry manifests in advance.

### Can Lokavia provide country-specific health certificates?
Yes. Depending on destination requirements, we provide health certificates, non-GMO declarations, radiation-free certificates, and Certificate of Origin legalized by the respective foreign embassy or Chamber of Commerce.

### What is the role of APEDA in Indian spice exports?
The Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA) is the statutory body under India's Ministry of Commerce responsible for export quality inspection and issuing official Registration-cum-Membership Certificates (RCMC).

### How is cargo insured under CIF shipments?
Under CIF agreements, Lokavia procures an Institute Cargo Clauses (A) "All Risks" marine insurance policy covering 110% of the total CIF value.
