## How Are Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs) Structured for Bulk Exports?

When procuring [Dehydrated Onion Powder](/products/onion-powder), [Garlic Powder](/products/garlic-powder), [Ginger Powder](/products/ginger-powder), or [Psyllium Husk](/products/psyllium-husk) from India, order volumes determine container utilization efficiency and freight cost per kilogram:

| Shipment Level | Volume Requirement | Container Loading Type | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trial / Sample Batch | 2,000 kg (2 Metric Tons) | LCL (Less than Container Load) | New product trial & lab bench testing |
| 20ft FCL Standard | 12,000 kg – 14,000 kg | Full Container Load (Palletized) | Regular distributor & medium manufacturing |
| 40ft HC High Capacity | 24,000 kg – 25,000 kg | Full Container Load (Floor / Pallet) | Industrial scale food processing plants |

Lokavia maintains flexible MOQ thresholds starting at **2,000 kg**, allowing global buyers to conduct pilot production runs before committing to full container load contracts.

## Which Incoterms 2020 Agreement Best Fits Your Logistics Operations?

![Agri Commodity Port Logistics and Shipping Containers](/blog/blog-bulk-sourcing.jpg)

Selecting the right commercial trade terms defines buyer and seller responsibilities for freight, insurance, and customs clearance:

- **FOB (Free on Board - Port of Loading):** Lokavia handles inland transport, customs clearance, and container loading at Mundra or Nhava Sheva port. Title and freight liability transfer once cargo passes over the ship's rail.
- **CIF (Cost, Insurance, & Freight - Destination Port):** Lokavia arranges ocean freight and maritime transit insurance to your designated sea port. Ideal for buyers seeking transparent landed port pricing.
- **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid):** End-to-end service where Lokavia manages import customs clearance, tariff payments, and door delivery directly to your factory warehouse.

## What Export Packaging Options Protect Cargo Quality During Transit?

Agri-commodity powders are highly hygroscopic and susceptible to moisture absorption if exposed to ambient air during transit. Standard industrial packaging includes:

1. **Multi-Wall Kraft Paper Bags (20 kg / 25 kg):** Heavy-duty 3-ply paper bags with an inner heat-sealed 80-micron food-grade polyethylene liner.
2. **Aluminum Foil Lined Bags:** Barrier packaging offering maximum protection against moisture, oxygen transmission, and volatile oil loss for high-gingerol and SO₂-free grades.
3. **FIBC Jumbo Totes (500 kg – 1,000 kg):** Woven polypropylene bulk bags with inner poly liners.
4. **Private Label & Custom Branding:** Custom outer carton printing, batch coding, and QR code lot tracking for ingredient distributors.

## How Does the Sample Evaluation and Ordering Process Work?

To ensure total alignment on product specifications before contract signing, [Lokavia](/about) follows a structured 4-step buyer onboarding process:

1. **Specification Matching:** Review your technical datasheet (mesh size, moisture, volatile oil, microbiological limits).
2. **Air Express Sampling:** Dispatch 200g–500g representative laboratory samples with batch COA via DHL/FedEx within 48 hours.
3. **Lab & Factory Approval:** Buyer performs bench-scale trials, moisture analysis, and sensory evaluation.
4. **Contract Execution & Dispatch:** Finalize FOB/CIF terms, issue Proforma Invoice, and schedule production dispatch from our Gujarat/Madhya Pradesh sourcing hubs.
