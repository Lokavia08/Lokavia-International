## What a COA Verifies in Industrial Spice Procurement

A COA provides certified analytical proof for each production lot, safeguarding against:
1. **Pneumatic Caking & Line Downtime:** High moisture causes powder agglomeration in automated auger fillers.
2. **Flavor Deficiencies:** Low volatile oil in ginger or deficient allicin in garlic leads to weak finished food flavor profiles.
3. **Customs Rejection & Detention:** Exceeding destination Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs) or heavy metals can trigger border destruction orders.

## 1. Physical Parameters & Granulometry

| Parameter | Standard Test Method | Onion Powder | Garlic Powder | Ginger Powder | Commercial Significance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mesh / Particle Size** | ASTM / US Sieve | 95%+ through 80/100 mesh | 95%+ through 80/100 mesh | 98%+ through 80/120 mesh | Dictates dissolution rate, dusting, and texture in dry rubs and batters |
| **Color & Appearance** | Visual / HunterLab | Off-white to pale cream | Pale cream to light tan | Light buff to golden yellow | Visual check for scorching or enzymatic browning during drying |
| **Bulk Density** | Tapped / Poured (g/mL) | 0.55 – 0.70 g/mL | 0.60 – 0.75 g/mL | 0.45 – 0.60 g/mL | Critical for volumetric recipe dosing and packaging fill levels |
| **Extraneous Matter** | Visual Floatation | < 0.5% max | < 0.5% max | < 0.5% max | Measures plant skin or rootlet fragments |
| **Foreign Matter** | X-Ray / Magnetic Trap | Nil (0.0%) | Nil (0.0%) | Nil (0.0%) | Zero tolerance for metal, glass, plastic, or stone fragments |

## 2. Chemical & Physicochemical Benchmarks

* **Moisture Content (% by mass):** Standard export threshold is ≤ 6.0% (Onion & Garlic) and ≤ 9.0% (Ginger) via Karl Fischer titration or vacuum oven drying (ASTA Method 2.0).
* **Total Ash & Acid-Insoluble Ash (AIA):** Total ash measures mineral residue after incineration at 550°C (max 5.0%). Acid-Insoluble Ash measures **sand, silica, or soil contamination** (max 0.5% for onion/garlic; max 1.0% for ginger).
* **Bioactive & Volatile Potency:** 
  - Garlic powder should yield ≥ 3,500 – 4,500 µg/g allicin potential upon reconstitution.
  - Ginger powder should contain ≥ 1.5% – 2.5% volatile oil (ASTA Method 7.0 Clevenger distillation).
* **Sulfur Dioxide ($SO_2$):** Less than 10 ppm ($SO_2$-free). Non-sulfited spices avoid allergen warnings in Western export markets.

## 3. Microbiological Standards

| Microbiological Assay | Target Limit (Standard Industrial) | Strict RTE / Baby Food Grade | Standard Test Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Total Plate Count (TPC)** | < 100,000 CFU/g | < 10,000 CFU/g | ISO 4833 / FDA BAM Ch. 3 |
| **Yeast and Mold Count** | < 1,000 CFU/g | < 100 CFU/g | ISO 21527 / FDA BAM Ch. 18 |
| **Coliforms** | < 100 CFU/g | < 10 CFU/g | ISO 4832 / AOAC 991.14 |
| **Escherichia coli (E. coli)** | < 10 CFU/g (Absent / g) | Absent in 10 g | ISO 16649 / FDA BAM Ch. 4 |
| **Salmonella spp.** | **Absent in 25 g** (or 375 g composite) | **Absent in 375 g** | ISO 6579 / AOAC 2013.01 |

## 4. Heavy Metals & Pesticide MRL Screening

1. **Heavy Metals (ICP-MS):** Lead ($Pb \le 1.0\text{ ppm}$), Arsenic ($As \le 0.5\text{ ppm}$), Cadmium ($Cd \le 0.5\text{ ppm}$), Mercury ($Hg \le 0.1\text{ ppm}$).
2. **Multi-Residue Pesticide Screen:** Testing across 500+ compounds via GC-MS/MS and LC-MS/MS ensuring compliance with EU Regulation 396/2005 and US EPA tolerances.
3. **Ethylene Oxide (ETO) & 2-Chloroethanol:** Combined limit < 0.02–0.05 mg/kg for EU compliance.
4. **Mycotoxins:** Aflatoxins total $\le 4.0\text{ ppb}$, Ochratoxin A $\le 15.0\text{ ppb}$.

## Common COA Red Flags to Audit

1. **Theoretical Round Numbers:** Generic numbers (e.g. exactly 5.00% moisture across several shipments) indicates a static spec sheet rather than an authentic batch test.
2. **Missing Standardized Test Methods:** Legitimate COAs explicitly reference ISO, AOAC, or ASTA protocols.
3. **Missing Accreditation Stamp:** Third-party reports must include NABL / ISO 17025 accreditation marks.
4. **Lot Number Inconsistencies:** The COA lot number must match outer master carton shipping marks and the Bill of Lading exactly.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the difference between a Product Specification Sheet and a COA?
A Product Specification Sheet outlines target parameters and allowable tolerances agreed before ordering. A Certificate of Analysis (COA) reports the actual empirical laboratory test results of a specific finished production lot before shipment.

### Does Lokavia provide third-party independent lab COAs?
Yes. In addition to internal factory QC analysis, Lokavia provides third-party testing from NABL-accredited international laboratories (Eurofins, SGS, Intertek) covering pesticide MRLs, heavy metals, ETO, and microbiological parameters upon request.

### What is the maximum allowable moisture in dehydrated onion and garlic powder?
The international export standard is ≤ 6.0% moisture by mass. Moisture levels below 5.5% are ideal for high-humidity destination ports to prevent moisture migration and lump formation.

### How do I verify whether garlic powder has been irradiated or steam-sterilized?
Steam sterilization is confirmed through microbiological clean-count reports alongside negative test results for irradiation (Photostimulated Luminescence / PSL and Thermoluminescence / TL under EN 1788 or EN 13751 standards).
