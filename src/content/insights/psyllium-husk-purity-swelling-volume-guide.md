## What Is Psyllium Husk & Why Does Purity Matter?

Psyllium husk is the clean, dried epidermis removed from the seeds of *Plantago ovata*, a plant native to Western India (primarily Gujarat and Rajasthan). India produces over **85% of the world's total psyllium supply**.

The husk consists of **80% soluble fiber**, forming a viscous mucilaginous gel when mixed with water. This unique property makes psyllium indispensable in:

1. **Nutraceuticals & Dietary Supplements:** Laxative formulations, cholesterol-lowering supplements, and IBS management.
2. **Gluten-Free & Keto Baking:** Acts as a binder and structural crumb mimic in gluten-free breads and pizzas.
3. **Animal Nutrition & Equine Health:** Prevents sand accumulation in horses' digestive systems.

Purity determines the ratio of pure white translucent husk husk relative to heavy dark seed coat fragments (known as "heavy seed" or "extraneous matter"). Higher purity yields greater swelling capacity and cleaner appearance.

## 2. Understanding Swelling Volume (Swelling Index Test)

Swelling volume is the definitive laboratory metric for psyllium husk potency. It measures the volume in milliliters occupied by **1 gram of psyllium husk** after expanding in water for 24 hours.

### Swelling Volume Testing Method (USP Monograph):
1. **Sample Preparation:** Accurately weigh 1.00 g of psyllium husk sample.
2. **Hydration:** Transfer to a 100 ml graduated cylinder containing 90 ml of distilled water.
3. **Agitation:** Shake vigorously every 10 minutes for 1 hour, then allow to stand undisturbed for 24 hours at room temperature.
4. **Measurement:** Read the volume occupied by the swollen mucilage gel layer.

* **Acceptable Standard:** Food-grade psyllium must achieve a minimum swelling volume of **40 ml/g**.
* **Pharma Standard:** USP/EP monograph grade psyllium must achieve **40 to 55+ ml/g**.
