## What Is Psyllium Husk & Why Does Purity Matter?

Psyllium husk is the clean, dried epidermis removed from the seeds of *Plantago ovata*, grown primarily in Gujarat and Rajasthan (supplying >85% of world volume). The husk yields over **80% soluble fiber**, forming a viscous mucilaginous gel in aqueous systems.

Purity measures the ratio of pure white translucent husk relative to heavy dark seed coat fragments and extraneous matter.

## 2. Swelling Volume Testing (USP Monograph)

Swelling volume measures the milliliters occupied by **1 gram of psyllium husk** after expanding in water:
1. **Weighing:** Accurately weigh 1.00 g of sample.
2. **Hydration:** Add to a 100 ml graduated cylinder with 90 ml of distilled water.
3. **Agitation:** Shake vigorously every 10 minutes for 1 hour; allow to stand undisturbed for 24 hours.
4. **Reading:** Read the volume occupied by the swollen mucilage layer. Minimum standard is 40 ml/g for food grade and 50–55+ ml/g for USP pharmaceutical grade.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the primary difference between 95% and 99% purity psyllium?
95% purity contains up to 5% light seed fragments and achieves 40–45 ml/g swelling volume (ideal for commercial baking), while 99% purity contains ≤1% extraneous matter and achieves 50–55 ml/g swelling volume for pharmaceutical use.

### Why is swelling volume important in gluten-free baking?
Higher swelling volumes yield stronger hydrocolloid gel networks that trap carbon dioxide bubbles, replicating the crumb structure and elasticity of gluten in breads and pizza doughs.

### Does steam sterilization lower the swelling volume?
When executed with precision high-temperature short-time (HT-ST) parameters, steam sterilization reduces microbiological counts while preserving 100% of the natural swelling index without chemical residues.

### What packaging is used for bulk psyllium export?
Psyllium husk is packed in 25 kg poly-lined multi-wall kraft paper bags or 500 kg jumbo sacks loaded on heat-treated ISPM-15 wooden pallets.
