## Why Botanical Ingredients Require Religious Auditing

In industrial spice milling and continuous dehydration lines, compliance risks stem from **processing aids, facility history, machinery lubricants, and cross-contact potential**:

1. **Machinery Lubricants & Greases:** Conveyors, dicers, and pin mills require lubricants. Halal and Kosher standards forbid animal-derived tallow or lard greases, requiring certified food-grade synthetic lubricants (NSF H1 registered).
2. **Boiler Steam & Sanitation:** Boiler steam additives and Clean-in-Place (CIP) sanitation agents must be free from ethyl alcohol and animal derivatives.
3. **Dedicated Equipment:** Facilities must prevent cross-contact with animal broths, dairy powders, or non-certified spice blends.

## GCC Halal Standards (UAE & Saudi Arabia)

* **GSO 2055-1 / UAE.S 2055-1:** General Requirements for Halal Food.
* **Accreditation Body Recognition:** Certifying bodies must be accredited by the **International Halal Accreditation Forum (IHAF)** or **SMIIC**, and registered with MoIAT/ESMA (UAE) or SFDA (Saudi Arabia).
* **Certificate Audit Points:** The certificate must explicitly list the exact product grades (Flakes, Kibbled, Minced, Granules, Powder), match the manufacturing plant address on the FSSAI license, and contain a scannable QR verification code.

## Kosher Certification for North American & European Markets

* **Kosher Pareve Classification:** Pure dehydrated onion and garlic powders are certified Kosher Pareve (neutral), containing neither meat nor dairy derivatives and processed on dedicated neutral equipment.
* **Kosher for Passover (Pesach):** Requires deep mechanical washdowns, rabbinical oversight, and zero processing contact with grain-based flours (*Chametz*) or kitniyot-derived starches.
* **Recognized Agencies:** Orthodox Union (OU), Star-K, OK Kosher, and KOF-K.
